03 / SELECTED CASE STUDIES

Complex systems.
Clearer outcomes.

Representative engagement patterns showing how I approach MarTech consolidation, demand operations and lifecycle activation.

These are anonymized, illustrative case-study narratives, not independently verified client testimonials. Specific client names, project scope and performance figures should only be added after approval and validation.

CASE 01MARTECH CONSOLIDATION

Global enterprise technology consolidation

Challenge

Multiple regional marketing platforms and legacy CRM instances can create duplicated operating costs, inconsistent lifecycle definitions and fragmented funnel reporting.

Approach

Assess the current-state stack, define a target architecture, standardize core data and lifecycle models, then plan migration waves with validation, governance and regional operating controls.

Outcome to measure

Platform cost, duplicate operational effort, migration data integrity, campaign execution consistency and global funnel visibility.

CASE 02B2B SAAS DEMAND ENGINE

Rebuilding the B2B demand engine

Challenge

High inbound activity does not automatically translate into qualified pipeline when scoring is weak, follow-up is inconsistent and sales SLAs are difficult to monitor.

Approach

Combine behavioral and profile signals, build segmented nurture tracks, define clear lifecycle transitions and create CRM/Slack alerts for SLA exceptions and high-intent activity.

Outcome to measure

MQL-to-SQL conversion, speed-to-lead, SLA adherence, opportunity progression and sales-cycle duration.

CASE 03LIFECYCLE PERSONALIZATION

From static campaigns to relevant journeys

Challenge

Broad campaign sends can miss buying context, lifecycle stage and account-level engagement signals.

Approach

Use consent-aware audience rules, behavioral triggers, suppression logic and reusable content tracks to make communications more relevant and measurable.

Outcome to measure

Engagement quality, conversion by segment, unsubscribe and complaint rates, influenced pipeline and journey completion.
